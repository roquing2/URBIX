1. Introduction

URBIX is an independent digital currency project focused on transparency, open-source development, technological independence and international community participation.

The project currently begins with a BEP-20 token deployed on BNB Smart Chain.

Its long-term vision is to research and potentially develop an independent blockchain network.

URBIX is an early-stage project. Its future blockchain, native coin and independent payment infrastructure have not yet been developed.

2. Mission and Vision

Mission

Develop an open and transparent digital currency project that encourages global collaboration and responsible technological innovation.

Vision

Explore the development of an independent blockchain infrastructure capable of supporting a decentralized digital currency ecosystem.

Core Principles

- Transparency.
- Open-source development.
- Security.
- Community participation.
- Technological independence.
- Responsible decentralization.

4. Token Distribution and Transparency

Transparency regarding token ownership and liquidity is fundamental to URBIX.

An analysis shared in November 2026 indicated approximately:

- 89.9% of the token supply held by the deployer wallet.
- 10% held in the decentralized exchange liquidity pool.
- The remaining portion held by other wallets.

These are approximate historical figures, not guaranteed current balances.

At the time of the referenced analysis, the liquidity provider tokens were controlled by the deployer wallet and were not locked or burned.

This means liquidity could be withdrawn by the wallet controlling those LP tokens.

URBIX does not claim that its current token distribution is decentralized.

Future changes to distribution and liquidity policies should be publicly documented.

5. Technical Architecture

Current Infrastructure

The existing token uses:

- BNB Smart Chain.
- BEP-20 token standard.
- An ERC-20-compatible smart contract architecture.

The existing token can be transferred using compatible blockchain wallets.

Future Research

A possible independent URBIX blockchain would require research and development in:

- Peer-to-peer networking.
- Consensus mechanisms.
- Transaction validation.
- Node infrastructure.
- Network synchronization.
- Wallet software.
- Block explorer.
- Network security.
- Scalability.

The consensus mechanism has not been selected.

Proof of Work and Proof of Stake are potential models for technical evaluation.

No independent URBIX blockchain is currently operational.

6. Intended Utility

URBIX intends to investigate digital payment use cases.

Potential areas of research include:

- Peer-to-peer digital transfers.
- Wallet integration.
- Merchant payment interfaces.
- QR-code payment workflows.
- Payment infrastructure.

These are development objectives, not existing commercial services.

The project does not currently operate an independent merchant payment network or guarantee acceptance by businesses.

7. Development Strategy

URBIX follows a progressive development strategy.

Stage 1 — Project Foundation

- Publish public documentation.
- Verify the existing token contract.
- Disclose token distribution and liquidity risks.
- Establish an open development repository.

Stage 2 — International Community

- Invite developers and researchers.
- Encourage independent technical reviews.
- Establish contribution guidelines.
- Publish development proposals.

Stage 3 — Blockchain Research

- Evaluate blockchain architectures.
- Compare consensus mechanisms.
- Define node requirements.
- Research wallet and explorer architecture.

Stage 4 — Experimental Network

Subject to available resources and successful research:

- Develop a blockchain prototype.
- Establish a public testnet.
- Test transactions and network synchronization.
- Invite independent testers.

Stage 5 — Security and Governance

- Conduct independent code reviews.
- Obtain professional security audits.
- Define transparent governance procedures.
- Establish decentralization requirements.

Stage 6 — Future Blockchain Evaluation

Only after successful testing, security reviews and legal analysis should a possible independent mainnet be considered.

9. Security

Security is a central development priority.

The existing contract has verified source code, but this does not establish that the complete project or future infrastructure is free from vulnerabilities.

Before any independent production blockchain is launched, the project intends to pursue:

- Independent technical reviews.
- Professional security audits.
- Public testing.
- Vulnerability reporting procedures.
- Incident response documentation.

10. Risks and Limitations

URBIX is an early-stage digital currency project.

Users and participants should understand the following risks:

Market risk: Token prices may fluctuate significantly.

Liquidity risk: Available liquidity may be limited or withdrawn.

Concentration risk: A substantial portion of the current token supply has been held by the deployer wallet.

Technical risk: Smart contracts, wallets and blockchain infrastructure may contain vulnerabilities.

Adoption risk: There is no guarantee that users, developers or merchants will adopt the project.

Regulatory risk: Digital currency regulations may affect the project's development and use.

Development risk: Future blockchain development may be delayed, changed or abandoned.

There is no guarantee of financial returns, appreciation, liquidity or future adoption.

11. Future Native Coin and Migration

The possible creation of a native URBIX coin is a future research objective.
